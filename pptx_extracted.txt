Business Strategy Who uses it? The product is a clinical decision-support layer between routine data and clinician attention. HOSPITALS & CLINICS Surface patients whose medication pattern may need review between appointments. PHARMACY / CARE NETWORKS Identify unexplained refill disruptions and prioritize evidence-based outreach. DIGITAL HEALTH Add longitudinal adherence-risk reasoning to existing patient-monitoring workflows. VALUE EARLIER SIGNALS Intermittent patterns can surface between visits. FEWER FALSE ALERTS Alternative-cause checks challenge simplistic conclusions. EXPLAINABLE Every alert is tied to observable evidence. SCALABLE Framework can extend across medicines and conditions.
